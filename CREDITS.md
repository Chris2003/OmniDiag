# Credits & AI disclosure

## Authorship

OmniDiag is roughly a **50 / 50** effort:

- **~50% AI-assisted.** AI coding assistants were used to draft and refactor code,
  write Pester tests, and produce documentation.
- **~50% human.** Chris B ([@Chris2003](https://github.com/Chris2003)) and other
  collaborators who worked **off platform** (outside GitHub, so they don't show in the
  commit history). People set the product direction and architecture, chose the
  technician workflows and safety boundaries, tested on real Windows endpoints, and
  reviewed and approved everything that shipped.

The split is an honest estimate of the overall effort, not a per-line measurement.

## Responsibility

All AI-generated output was reviewed before it was merged. The maintainers are
responsible for OmniDiag's behavior, as with any other contribution. Found a problem?
Please open an issue (or see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security reports).

## Third-party

- [Pester](https://pester.dev/) for testing.
- [Ollama](https://ollama.com/) and Google's Gemma models, both optional, for local AI analysis.
